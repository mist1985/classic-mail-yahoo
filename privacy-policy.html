Classic Mail Skin for Yahoo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Summary

Classic Mail Skin for Yahoo does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the extension does

It adds a bundled stylesheet to pages on mail.yahoo.com to change how Yahoo Mail looks. It does not read the content of your emails, contacts, or any other page content, and it makes no network requests other than loading its own bundled stylesheet from inside the extension.

Data stored

Only three display settings are saved: whether the classic look is on, the theme (Light, Dark or Auto), and whether preview text is shown. They are saved with the browser's built-in chrome.storage.sync, which your browser may sync between your own devices if you are signed in to it. The developer cannot access these settings.

Analytics, tracking and third parties

None. The extension contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or remotely hosted code, and no data is transferred to the developer or any third party.

Permissions

  • storage — to remember the three settings above.
  • Access to mail.yahoo.com — to apply the stylesheet on Yahoo Mail pages only.
